Public Talks and Observing

Every month (except for January), we host a public talk! These talks focus on an astronomy topic, are free, and are fun for all ages including kids. We'll start the night with trivia and announcements, hear from our speaker, have Q&A, and then end with a raffle of Columbia Astro merch.

If the weather is clear, we will host observing after the event.
